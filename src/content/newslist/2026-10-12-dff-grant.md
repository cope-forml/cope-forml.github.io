Amartya has been awarded **DKK 7,198,686** for **Data-Adaptive Privacy for Machine Learning** at the University of Copenhagen. The grant comes through [**Independent Research Fund Denmark (DFF)’s thematic call on research in artificial intelligence (2026–2029).**](https://dff.dk/en/our-funded-projects/see-an-overview-of-supported-research/?GoCaseId=6305-00038A)
