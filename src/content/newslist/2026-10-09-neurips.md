Congratulations to Johanna, Vikrant, and Christian on their NeurIPS 2026 acceptances! Johanna's [paper on adaptive private k-PCA](/publications/#optimal-dngler-2026) was selected as a spotlight. Vikrant's [paper on privately estimating monotone statistics](/publications/#privately-brown-2026) and Christian's [Lumberjack paper on differentially private random forests](/publications/#lumberjack-lebeda-2026) were also accepted.

Our paper on private question answering without private training was accepted to [InfPriv: The New Landscape of AI Privacy](https://beyond-private-training.ai.studio/), a workshop at NeurIPS 2026.
